# CS-255-System-Analysis-and-Design
Portfolio

## Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

I worked with a client called DriverPass on this project. DriverPass wanted to build a system to help students get ready for their driving test. Students needed a way to take practice tests, do online learning, and book driving lessons. The system also had to let customers make an account, pick a driving package, set up driving lessons, and see how much progress they had made.

## What did you do particularly well?

I think I did well at understanding what DriverPass wanted from the system and turning those needs into different requirements. I also think working through the diagrams helped me get a better understanding of how the different users would interact with the system. As I worked through the project, I started to see how the requirements from the beginning affected the design later on.

## If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

If I could go back and revise something, I would spend more time on my UML diagrams. Some of my diagrams did not use the correct UML symbols or relationships, and my class diagram could have included more information about the attributes. I would improve this by checking the UML rules more carefully and comparing each diagram to the requirements before finishing it.

## How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?

I used the DriverPass interview to figure out what the customers and employees needed the system to do. For example, customers needed to schedule driving lessons and take practice tests. Employees needed access to schedules, accounts, and reports. I used those needs when creating the requirements and diagrams. I think considering the customers' and employees' needs is important because there is no point in designing a system if the system does not actually solve the problems the client has.

## How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?

I learned that I should begin by understanding what is needed before trying to create the system. Once I have that, I can split the system into pieces and use diagrams to show how it works. In the future, I will take time to review my work as I do it instead of waiting until everything is done. I will also make sure my diagrams follow both the client's needs and the right UML rules.
